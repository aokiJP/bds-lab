## breaking blocks earns coins
@A join
@B join
setblock 2 -60 2 dirt
setblock 3 -60 2 dirt
@A dig 2 -60 2
@A dig 3 -60 2
wait 500
@A cmd /balance
~ @A cmd: A: 2 coins
## paying moves coins, with checks
@A cmd /pay B 1
~ @A cmd: paid 1 to B: 1 left
~ @B A sent you 1 coins
@A cmd /pay B 5
~ not enough coins \(1\)
@B cmd /balance
~ @B cmd: B: 1 coins
## the accounts survive a restart
restart
@A cmd /balance
~ @A cmd: A: 1 coins
