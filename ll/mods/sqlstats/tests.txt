## blocks a player breaks are counted in SQLite
@A join
setblock 2 -60 2 dirt
setblock 3 -60 2 dirt
@A dig 2 -60 2
@A dig 3 -60 2
wait 500
@A cmd /stats
~ @A cmd: A: blocks 2 joins 1
## the database survives a restart
restart
@A cmd /top
~ @A cmd: top blocks: 1\. A 2
@A cmd /stats A
~ @A cmd: A: blocks 2 joins 2
