## each player is greeted in their own game language
@A join locale=en_US
~ ^@A Welcome, A!
@B join locale=ja_JP
~ ^@B ようこそ、Bさん！
@C join locale=es_MX
~ ^@C ¡Bienvenido, C!
## /rules answers in the player's language
@B cmd /rules
~ ^@B .*ルール: 1\. やさしく
@A cmd /rules
~ ^@A .*Rules: 1\. Be kind
## one /announce reaches everyone, each in their language
announce restart
~ announced restart to 3 players
wait 1000
~ ^@A .*The server restarts in 5 minutes
~ ^@B .*5分後にサーバーを再起動します
~ ^@C .*El servidor se reinicia en 5 minutos
## an unknown language falls back to English
@D join locale=xx_XX
~ ^@D Welcome, D!
## /lang overrides the game language and is remembered after a restart
@A cmd /lang de
~ ^@A .*Sprache: de
restart
@A cmd /rules
~ ^@A .*Regeln: 1\. Sei nett
@A cmd /lang auto
~ ^@A .*Language: en
## wrong input is refused politely
@A cmd /lang zz
~ languages: auto en ja
announce nope
~ keys: restart event
