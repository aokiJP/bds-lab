## the held pickaxe gets the Unbreakable tag and keeps it
@A join
give A iron_pickaxe
@A select iron_pickaxe
@A cmd /unbreakable
~ @A cmd: minecraft:iron_pickaxe is unbreakable
@A cmd /nbt
~ @A cmd: .*"?Unbreakable"?:1b
## it really does not wear: digging leaves its damage at 0
setblock 2 -60 2 stone
setblock 3 -60 2 stone
@A dig 2 -60 2
@A dig 3 -60 2
wait 500
lse return p('A').getHand().getNbt().toSNBT().includes('Damage:0') || !/Damage:[1-9]/.test(p('A').getHand().getNbt().toSNBT())
= true
