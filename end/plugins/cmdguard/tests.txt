## a muted player can neither chat nor whisper (/tell /msg /w /me)
@A join
@B join
mute B 5
~ B muted for 5 min
@B chat hello everyone
~ ^@B .*You are muted \(\d+s left\)
!~ ^@A .*hello everyone
@B cmd /tell A secret
!~ ^@A .*secret
@B cmd /msg A secret2
!~ ^@A .*secret2
@B cmd /me waves
!~ ^@A .*waves
~ ^@B .*You are muted
## other players still talk, and /unmute gives the voice back
@A chat hi B
~ ^@B .*hi B
unmute B
~ B can talk again
@B chat back again
~ ^@A .*back again
