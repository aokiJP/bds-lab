## loads on BDS and asks a new player to start
@A join
~ tsrepl_start
!~ requesting dependency

## start gives the console and opens the workbench
@A join
wait 2000
@A cmd /function tsrepl_start
until form
~ console1
~ tsrepl.start.done
~ form action: title\("ワークベンチ +TS REPL"\)
@A form close

## doctor answers the player who asked
@A join
wait 2000
@A cmd /function tsrepl_doctor
until 診断
~ TS REPL の診断 +v6\.11\.0

## the console item opens the workbench
@A join
wait 2000
@A cmd /function tsrepl_start
until form
@A form close
wait 300
@A select tsrepl:console
@A use
until form
~ form action: title\("ワークベンチ +TS REPL"\)
@A form close

## a script block with nothing bound opens its binding screen
@A join
wait 2000
@A cmd /function tsrepl_start
until form
@A form close
wait 300
give A tsrepl:script_block
wait 500
@A place tsrepl:script_block 2 -60 2
wait 500
@A useon 2 -60 2
until スクリプトブロック
~ form action: title\("ワークベンチ +スクリプトブロック"\).*button\(" *2, -60, 2"\)
@A form close
