## the first join of the day gives one emerald
@A join
~ @A Login bonus: 1 emerald \(day 1 in a row\)
wait 300
js inv(p('A'))
= ["0:minecraft:emerald*1"]

## a second join on the same day gives nothing
@A leave
@A join
wait 500
!~ Login bonus
js inv(p('A'))
= ["0:minecraft:emerald*1"]

## the next day counts the streak, and it survives a restart
clock +1d
restart
~ @A Login bonus: 1 emerald \(day 2 in a row\)

## the 7th day in a row gives five
clock +1d
@A leave
@A join
clock +1d
@A leave
@A join
clock +1d
@A leave
@A join
clock +1d
@A leave
@A join
clock +1d
@A leave
@A join
~ @A Login bonus: 5 emerald \(day 7 in a row\)

## a missed day starts over
clock +2d
@A leave
@A join
~ @A Login bonus: 1 emerald \(day 1 in a row\)
