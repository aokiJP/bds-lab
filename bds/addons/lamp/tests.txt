## loads
@A join
~ joined

## touching the lamp switches it
setblock 2 -60 2 lab:lamp
@A interact 2 -60 2
~ @A Lamp on
@A interact 2 -60 2
~ @A Lamp off

## a nickname survives a restart
@A cmd /lab:nick
until form
@A form ["Steve"]
~ @A Nickname set: Steve
restart
@A cmd /lab:whoami
~ @A You are Steve
