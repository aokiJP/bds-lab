# command, then expectations: = exact line | ~ regex | ! absent | !~ absent regex; '## title' = section
## the demo form reaches a real client
@A join
scriptevent demo:open
until form
~ @A form action: title\(".*JSON UI Demo"\) body\("This form is drawn
@A form Emerald
~ DEMO A picked emerald
## a player opens it with /lab:menu
@A cmd /lab:menu
until form
~ @A form action: title\(".*JSON UI Demo"\) body\("This form is drawn
@A form close
~ DEMO A picked closed
## the console opens it for every player with /lab:menuall
@A join
@B join
wait 1000
lab:menuall
~ DEMO shown to 2
wait 1500
~ @A form action: title\(".*JSON UI Demo"\)
~ @B form action: title\(".*JSON UI Demo"\)
@A form close
@B form close
~ DEMO B picked closed
